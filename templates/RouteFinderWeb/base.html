🗺️ Welcome to RouteFinder

Plan smarter garage sale and thrift store trips in minutes. Find nearby listings, spot the best stops, and get an optimized driving route.

🔍 1. Find Stops

Head to the Discover tab, enter your Zip Code and Radius, and switch between Garage Sales and Thrift Stores.

  • ✔️ Live Listings: Pulls current local sales automatically.
  • ✔️ Save for Later: Bookmark your favorite stops ahead of the weekend.

✨ 2. Smart Highlights

When AI is enabled in your Profile, listings are tagged to help you prioritize:

⚠️ Low Priority: Listing looks thin or unlikely to match what you're after.
✨ Notable Finds: High-interest items mentioned in the description.
🎯 Wishlist Match: Matches items you've added to your personal wishlist.

🚗 3. Build Your Route

Select the stops you want to visit and click Import. RouteFinder calculates the fastest order, factoring in drive time and how long you plan to spend at each stop.

You're all set to start planning.
